2026-08-20 — What I Did Today ​

  • 日期：2026-08-20
  • 主题：回顾今天做了什么
  • 录音时长：约 4 分 52 秒
  • 练习形式：无脚本自由复述

已经出现的进步 ​

  • 时间线完整清楚：从起床、出门、通勤、工作、午饭、会议、下班， 一直讲到晚餐和学习英语，听者能够理解一天的先后顺序。
  • 没有依赖原剧情台词，仍然表达了约 180 词。相比背熟固定复述，这更能 暴露真实的自由表达能力。
  • 时间信息很具体：8:20、9:30、12 o'clock、5:30、6:00、 6:30 和 9 p.m. 都参与了叙事，没有只说模糊的早上和晚上。
  • could not take time off 一类影视表达之外，本次主动使用了真实生活词 汇：刷牙、电脑、背包、地铁、开会、点餐和奶茶。
  • 结尾有明确收束：吃完晚餐和奶茶以后，晚上九点学习英语，故事没有在 中途突然停止。

当前主要问题 ​

  • 停顿仍是第一问题：约 16 处超过 3 秒的停顿，其中约 6 处超过 5 秒。卡顿集中在出门前的动作串联、到公司后的安排、午饭转到下午 工作，以及回家后点餐的表达。
  • 一般过去时不稳定。前面已经使用 woke up、brushed、went 和 arrived，后面又滑回 buy、use、have、leave、order、 get 和 learn。
  • 过度依赖 and then。它能保证顺序，但每一段都用同一个连接词，会让 叙述显得机械，也没有帮助建立“上班途中”“午饭以后”“回家路上”等 更大的时间块。
  • 很多停顿发生在句子已经开始以后，说明开口前没有先选定谓语。例如想 说乘地铁时先说了 I need to...，随后才寻找 take。
  • company 被反复用于“去公司、到公司、离开公司”。日常英语中使用 go to work、get to the office 和 leave the office 更自然。
  • 13 p.m. 和 9 o'clock p.m. 混用了两种时间制。应说 13:00 或 1 p.m.，以及 9 p.m. 或 9 o'clock。
  • 午饭和晚饭发生了词汇串线：回家后想表达吃晚餐，机器多次识别成 have lunch。需要把 order dinner → eat dinner 练成一个语块。

以下发音可以练习，但不能只凭机器结果直接判错：

  • alarm clock：两个模型都没有稳定识别，注意 alarm 的第二音节重读， 以及 clock 的起始辅音。
  • worked / working：多次被听成 walking，注意 work 中的元音和 结尾 /k/。
  • milk tea：被听成 meal tea，注意保留 milk 的 /k/ 再连接 tea。

本次暴露的表达问题 ​

1. woke up by alarm clock ​

原表达：

text
I woke up at 8:20 by my alarm clock.

更自然的表达：

text
I woke up at 8:20 when my alarm went off.

也可以说：

text
My alarm woke me up at 8:20.

2. put my computer into my bag ​

如果这里说的是笔记本电脑，日常表达用 laptop 更自然：

text
I put my laptop in my bag.

想表达完整的出门动作，可以说：

text
I packed my laptop, put on my backpack, and left home.

3. have the 13th subway ​

原表达：

text
I need to have the 13th subway to go to my company.

建议：

text
I took Line 13 to work.
  • 地铁线路使用 Line 13，不是 the 13th subway。
  • 讲已经发生的事情时使用 took。

4. arrived at my company ​

原表达可以理解，但更自然的是：

text
I got to the office at 9:30.

或者：

text
I arrived at work at 9:30.

5. buy something before I arrived ​

原表达：

text
I buy something before I arrived at my company.

建议：

text
I bought something for breakfast on my way to the office.

buy 的过去式是 bought。

6. first use my computer until 12 ​

原表达：

text
I first use my computer until 12 o'clock.

建议：

text
I worked on my computer until noon.

这里重点是工作，不需要用 use my computer 绕开核心动词。

7. went on working at 13 p.m. ​

建议：

text
After lunch, I went back to work at 1 p.m.

也可以说：

text
I resumed work at 1 p.m.

8. a meeting from 5:30 to 6 ​

原表达缺少谓语：

text
A meeting from 5:30 p.m. to 6 o'clock p.m.

建议：

text
The meeting lasted from 5:30 to 6 p.m.

9. leave my company ​

原表达：

text
I leave my company at 6:30 p.m.

建议：

text
I left the office at 6:30 p.m.

10. after 13th subway to went home ​

建议直接说：

text
I took Line 13 home.

11. order dinner so I can eat after I get home ​

原表达：

text
I use my phone to order dinner on the train, so I can eat immediately
after I get home.

建议保持过去视角：

text
I ordered dinner on my phone while I was on the train, so I could eat
as soon as I got home.

12. order a milk tea ​

原表达：

text
I order a milk tea called Bawang Chaji.

如果说的是品牌，更自然的表达是：

text
I also ordered a milk tea from Bawang Chaji.

13. learn English at 9 o'clock p.m. ​

原表达：

text
I learn English at 9 o'clock p.m.

建议：

text
I practiced English at 9 p.m.

learn English 强调长期学习；描述当晚进行的一次活动时，practiced English 更具体。

修正后的完整版本 ​

text
Today I woke up at 8:20 when my alarm went off. I brushed my teeth,
packed my laptop in my bag, put on my backpack, and left home. Then I
walked to the subway station and took Line 13 to work. I got to the
office at 9:30. On my way there, I bought something for breakfast.

I worked on my computer until noon. After lunch, I went back to work at
1 p.m. Later, I had a meeting from 5:30 to 6 p.m. I left the office at
6:30 and took Line 13 home.

While I was on the train, I ordered dinner on my phone so I could eat
as soon as I got home. The dinner was delicious. I also ordered a milk
tea from Bawang Chaji, and it was delicious too. Finally, I practiced
English at 9 p.m. That was my day.

想不起准确表达时的降级说法 ​

  • 地铁站名 → the subway station near my home
  • Line 13 → the subway
  • 早餐名称 → something for breakfast
  • went back to work → started working again
  • the meeting lasted... → the meeting was from... to...
  • ordered dinner on my phone → bought dinner online
  • Bawang Chaji → a milk tea shop

下一轮训练方法 ​

1. 使用八格时间线 ​

text
8:20 wake up → get ready
leave home → Line 13
9:30 office → breakfast
work → noon lunch → 1 p.m.
5:30 meeting → 6:30 leave
Line 13 home → order dinner
dinner → milk tea
9 p.m. → practice English

每格只说一到两句，讲完一格以后再进入下一格，不在句子中途寻找下一个 情节点。

2. 复述前只看过去时谓语轨道 ​

text
woke up → brushed → packed → left → took → arrived → bought
worked → had → resumed → left → ordered → ate → practiced

完整说 3 遍，每次只检查动词，不追求增加新细节。

3. 替换重复的 and then ​

下一轮必须至少使用下面四个时间连接语：

text
On my way to work...
After lunch...
Later that afternoon...
On my way home...

4. 下一次量化目标 ​

  1. 保留八个时间块，机器识别词数控制在 140～180 词。
  2. 总时长控制在 3 分钟以内。
  3. 超过 3 秒的停顿不多于 8 次。
  4. 超过 5 秒的停顿不多于 2 次。
  5. 不出现 buy、use、have、leave、order 和 learn 等现在时。
  6. 稳定使用 took Line 13、got to the office 和 left the office。
  7. 至少使用四个不同的时间连接语。
