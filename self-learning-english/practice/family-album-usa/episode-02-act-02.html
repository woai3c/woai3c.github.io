已经出现的进步 ​

  • 换成新材料后仍然能够独立讲完整个故事，主线从到达餐厅一直延续到 Harry 回家。
  • 单位时间表达的信息更多，整体语速从约 39 词/分钟提高到约 51 词/分钟。
  • 卡顿次数暂时没有减少，但超过 5 秒、接近完全断线的停顿明显减少。
  • Harry、Susan、Michelle、babysitter、restaurant 和 stomachache 等核心词识别相对稳定。
  • 已经能主动使用 because、so、but 和 after 推进故事，不再只是罗列零散信息。

当前主要问题 ​

  • 停顿仍然频繁，尤其集中在人物介绍、点餐、从点餐过渡到聊天，以及 Harry 道歉后的结尾部分。
  • 内容增加以后，过去时容易滑回一般现在时：need、don't care、care、there's 等。
  • 有些词虽然意思接近，但放在情境中会产生错误含义。例如 didn't care 是“不在乎”，不能表示“不需要菜单”。
  • 仍然倾向于边想复杂句边说，导致句子中间失去结构。

本次暴露的表达问题 ​

1. Thailand restaurant ​

原表达：

text
Harry and Susan run to the Thailand restaurant.

建议：

text
Harry and Susan went to a Thai restaurant.
  • Thailand 是国家名，修饰餐厅时使用 Thai。
  • 复述过去发生的故事时使用 went。

2. need to care about Harry ​

原表达：

text
Because Harry was here the first time, Susan need to care about Harry.

建议：

text
Since it was Harry's first time there, Susan helped him order.

care about Harry 表示关心 Harry，不等于帮助他点餐。直接说具体动作更清楚。

3. didn't care about that ​

原表达：

text
Harry didn't care about that.

建议：

text
Harry said he didn't need a menu.

didn't care 表示不在乎；想表达“不需要”时使用 didn't need。

4. talking about anything ​

原表达：

text
They're talking about anything. About job, about Harry's daughter.

建议：

text
They talked about their jobs and Harry's daughter.

这里已经知道具体话题，因此不使用 anything。

5. a phone call coming ​

原表达：

text
There's a phone call coming.

建议：

text
Then Harry got a phone call from the babysitter.

6. Susan don't care ​

原表达：

text
Susan don't care about that. She care about Michelle.

建议：

text
Susan didn't mind. She was worried about Michelle.

didn't mind 表示 Susan 不介意约会被打断；was worried about 表示她担心 Michelle。

7. another reservation last time ​

原表达：

text
They can make another reservation last time.

建议：

text
They agreed to see each other another time.

这里想表达的是以后再约会，不只是重新订餐厅座位。

关于“词汇量 6000～7000 仍然找不到词” ​

词汇量测试通常测量的是看到单词后能够认出的被动词汇。脱稿复述需要的是在一两秒内主动提取单词，并把它放进正确的句型中，两者不是同一种能力。

复述时还需要同时回忆剧情、选择时态、组织句子和规划下一段内容。工作记忆负担增大以后，即使是已经认识的高频词，也可能暂时提取不出来。

当前核心问题不是继续扩大词汇表，而是把少量高频叙事句块练到能够自动调用：

text
went to a Thai restaurant
knew the owner
helped him order
got a phone call from the babysitter
had a stomachache and was crying
apologized and said he had to leave
was worried about Michelle
agreed to see each other another time

修正后的复述 ​

text
In Act 2, Harry and Susan went to a Thai restaurant. Susan knew the
owner, Somsak, and introduced him to Harry. Since it was Harry's first
time there, Susan helped him order. They both ordered mee krob. Susan
had ginger ale, and Harry ordered a glass of white wine. Somsak also
recommended a special salad.

While they were waiting for the food, Harry and Susan talked about
their jobs and Harry's daughter, Michelle. Then Harry got a phone call
from the babysitter. She said Michelle had a stomachache and was
crying. Harry apologized to Susan and said he had to go home. Susan
didn't mind. She was worried about Michelle and told Harry to go home
right away. They agreed to see each other another time.

下一轮训练方法 ​

  1. 只看情节关键词，先用 8 个简单句讲完整主线。
  2. 想不起专有词时立即使用上面的降级表达，不等待精确词。
  3. 一个情节点只说一个句子，全程优先使用一般过去时。
  4. 第一次将总时长控制在 2 分 30 秒以内，之后再缩短到 2 分钟左右。
  5. 最终目标是超过 3 秒的停顿不多于 3 次。
