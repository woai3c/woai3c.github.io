容易混淆或误用的词 ​

只记录真实训练中搞混过的词。

found / founded ​

  • found：find 的过去式，意思是"找到"
    • He found the address after asking for directions.
  • founded：found 的过去式，意思是"创立、建立"
    • The company was founded in 2010.

babysitter / baby sister ​

  • babysitter：保姆，照看小孩的人
    • Harry wanted to leave the phone number with the babysitter.
  • baby sister：小妹妹，婴儿妹妹
    • She has a baby sister who is only two months old.
